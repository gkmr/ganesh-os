Step 1 · The loop

Everything flows one way, then back to the human

No agent talks to another agent directly. They coordinate through files in one shared store, and every message to the operator goes through one relay that logs each send. That log, not an HTTP code, is the truth of what was delivered.

⏰Scheduled agentsbriefs, scanners, boards, coaches, watchdogs, each on its own cron
🗄Shared storestate files, digests, cards, run markers - append-only, versioned
📡Delivery relayindependent infrastructure; posts to the messenger, logs every send
📱The phonea main feed and an ops feed; rich HTML cards ride along
🧑‍✈️Plain English backreplies from any surface become task, calendar, and state writes

🔒 Single-writer law

Every mutable field has exactly one owning agent, enforced by a behavioral eval over the change log. Two voices can never fight over one fact, so debugging is archaeology, not guesswork.

🧾 Verdict by ledger

"Sent" means a new line in the delivery ledger, nothing else. The transport lies (it returns errors on successful posts); the ledger does not. A missing evening digest was found and re-couriered in minutes because of this.

Step 2 · A day in the machine

Anchors speak. Preparers stay silent. Watchdogs bark once.

The day has a shape: two anchors the operator always reads, silent preparers that write files for them, and guards that only ever say something when something is wrong. Scroll the beats:

Pre-dawn
Write-only sweep · reconciles calendars, conflicts, and proposals into a file. Says nothing.
Early morning
Inbox scanner · reads ~200 emails with zero credentials in context, writes a digest plus a newsletter deep-dive card to the store.
7:00a
⌚ Wake line on the wrist · the same thought the morning brief opens with. One voice, two surfaces.
7:05a
💪 The morning anchor · computes sleep, recovery, strain, and mood from overnight health data; picks the day's state band; delivers the plan, the inbox bottom line, a ranked shortlist, and up to three color-coded focus blocks written back to the task store. Waits for late data instead of guessing.
Morning
💼 Role scanner · diffs target companies' careers pages, lands net-new fits in an intake list, silent when nothing is new, and backfills the whole gap after any outage.
Midday
📥 Needs-you check · humans waiting, hard deadlines, physical mail read by vision. Silent unless something truly needs the operator.
Evening
🌆 The wrap · recomputes the band from the day's actual strain, closes the loop on food and training, proposes tomorrow's plan as numbered, reply-able items.
Night
📋 The board · review queue, tomorrow's draft, a seven-day look-ahead that plans far but writes nothing beyond tomorrow, and per-domain link boards ranked with the calendar.
9:45p
🧾 The ops ledger · did all 30+ tasks run, deliver, or stay silent by law? One telemetry message to the ops feed, one heartbeat line to the main feed.
Step 3 · The adaptive tone engine

One thought, three registers, picked by the human's actual state

A weekly generator writes coaching lines in three variants; the daily briefs select one from live physiology plus the operator's own words, which always outrank the sensors. Tap a card.

🟢

CHARGED

strong recovery · good sleep · high mood

Register: forward, spend the energy.

Session language says go get the heavy set; the coach's call is ambitious; even the role digest's framing leans in.
🟡

STEADY

mixed signals · or data missing

Register: grounded, one foot in front.

Show up, honor the streak, quality over heroics. The default whenever the data cannot decide - the system never guesses.
🔴

RESTORE

red recovery · short sleep · or the human says so

Register: gentle. Rest is the strong move.

Never "push through", never guilt. Fewer asks everywhere; urgency never stacks. A bad number is reframed as agency, never scolded.

The design rule: tone flexes, content never does. Selection, ranking, and silence gates are band-independent.

🗣 The human outranks the sensors

A typed "wiped" or "red day" beats every computed score for the rest of the day, and one reply reshapes every remaining quote, session suggestion, and coaching call. Morning and evening recompute independently: a day can open charged and close restore.

🎨 Context that earns its place

Preambles may weave in at most one or two facts of the day's shape (calendar density, the planned session, tonight's plans). It reads like the system knows the day, never like a dashboard reciting itself.

Step 4 · Layered, not duplicated

One data substrate, three voices

Measurement, prescription, and spirit are separate jobs on the same numbers - computed once, by one writer, so they can never disagree about what kind of morning it is. Hover the stack.

💬 Spirit - the day's registerHow should today feel? One band-matched line that lands before business.
🎯 Prescription - the coachWhat should you do? One concrete action, real numbers, train-or-rest verdicts on demand.
📊 Measurement - the KPI engineWhat is your state? Sleep, recovery, strain, mood, computed once per morning by a single writer.
🗄 Data - exports plus the human's wordsWearable and nutrition streams, workout records, free-text check-ins.
Step 5 · Reliability culture

Incidents become laws, not apologies

Every production failure ends as a named, dated law inside the offending agent's own instructions, with the incident as its reason. A sample from one week:

Marker-first

Two guards died silently before their only write. Now every guard's first action is writing "run started" to its own log, so a half-written marker names exactly where a run died.

Courier self-audit

A courier skipped two undelivered cards without noticing. Now every run must record "delivered N" or "nothing pending (checked)", and the nightly ledger flags the line's absence the same night.

The watchdog outside the walls

Every monitor once lived inside the environment it monitored, so outages killed the watchdogs with their patients. The alarm now lives on independent infrastructure and texts the operator directly when the fleet goes quiet.

No fabricated links, ever

A status message once referenced a file with a dead link. Law: every reference carries its real captured URL or renders as plain text. Applies to job postings, tasks, and the system's own artifacts alike.

Step 6 · Human sovereignty

The machine proposes. The human disposes.

HUMAN-EDIT-WINS · a hand edit freezes its rank for 7 days, never counter-ordered Propose-only boards · numbered items, one-word replies, appliers do the writes Confirmed ordering · when priorities genuinely contend, the system asks once and remembers the answer Irreversible = human-gated · nothing is sent or deleted without an explicit instruction Signal law · silence is a valid, provable output; markers prove quiet means quiet

Replies work from any surface in plain English, are acknowledged exactly once, applied by exactly one lane, and visible in the next brief. The interface is a conversation; the guarantees are systems engineering.

What transfers

If your org is building with agents

The portable lessons: give every fact one owner before you scale writers; treat delivery as a ledger problem, not a transport problem; make silence provable; put monitors outside the failure domain; let tone adapt to the user while content stays deterministic; and gate the irreversible on a human. This repo documents each pattern with the incident that taught it - the operator ran the postmortems, wrote the evals, and shipped the fixes personally.
